# Independent assessment

Assess the selected engineering responsibility against its governing basis and enough actual context to judge the complete declared scope. Requirement Review assesses obligations and justified reuse; integrated Design Review assesses logical behavior and architecture together; Delivery Review assesses sequencing and proof; whole-change Code Review assesses the delivered implementation and interactions. Advisory code assessment supplies useful findings without formal approval.

Identify actual inspected subjects, reviewer, contributors and independence basis. Authors do not silently repair subjects and independently approve those repairs. A known author cannot supply independent approval of their own implementation. Labels and matching hashes cannot authenticate independence or establish semantic adequacy.

Record actionable findings with scope, evidence, required outcome, accountable owner and a safe correction path. Return defects to the owning requirement, system-design, architecture-design, planning or implementation responsibility. A reviewer records judgments and issue dispositions; route and storage cannot manufacture them. Omission never resolves an existing concern.

Formal judgments are approved, changes-requested, blocked or inconclusive. Advisory judgment is null. Approval does not automatically establish current applicability, authorize external action or prove final completion. Record the support and limitations needed for current reliance, without requiring a permanent report for every intermediate attempt.

For corrections, assess changed subjects and affected interactions, reuse justified unaffected coverage, and ensure the current whole scope remains adequately assessed. Earlier judgments retain their original meaning. Preserve current support gaps and renew reliance explicitly. Separate final Verify remains required after whole-change approval.
