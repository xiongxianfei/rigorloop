# Assessment reliance and closeout application

This packaged guidance applies explicitly adopted Review and Closeout policy (RC-SR-05–18), owned within the project's Workflow domain. It applies that policy without becoming a second owner or requiring RigorLoop's internal Design repository. Select the project contract first: installing guidance does not adopt it or migrate historical records. Keep historical procedures, identities, vocabularies and approvals truthful.

## Current reliance and correction

Before relying on a judgment or proof, inspect its exact required basis, scope, independence when required, concerns and relevant new evidence. Omitted, missing, stale, contradictory or unknown required basis prevents reliance. Matching hashes alone do not establish applicability. Required evidence must remain understandable from current project artifacts without Git, PR, network or chat history.

When engineering subjects change, the author declares assessment/proof impact, restricts affected applicability and identifies correction needs. Any responsible actor may conservatively restrict applicability; only the responsible assessor renews its assessment. Route cannot restore another actor's approval. Revised engineering work requires appropriate independent reassessment. Ambiguous or concurrent impact needs an owned blocker, not guessed validity or retargeted old judgment.

Classify recording-only changes by content and effect. A review save, blocker entry or successful explanation does not automatically invalidate unchanged engineering work because storage bytes changed. New contradictory evidence, altered scope or changed engineering subjects still require assessment. Preserve original reviewed identities and CLI conflict/recovery checks; no pathname exemption or unsafe replay follows from bookkeeping classification.

The correction owner repairs; the reporter assesses disposition with rationale and proof. A later clean review cannot close another actor's concern. In particular, Verify rechecks and dispositions its own blocker after correction and appropriate independent reassessment. Defects after completion remain recordable and defeat contradicted completion claims. Interrupted or failed recording stops reliance and names the recovery owner; a successful save alone grants no judgment or progression.

## Evidence reuse

Reuse requires an existing pass, known proved surfaces, current governing authority and subject/environment identity, affirmative evidence that those surfaces are unaffected, and no freshness override. New assessment alone does not require all commands to rerun. Changed subjects, procedure, dependencies, environment or relevant evidence require new proof when they defeat the reuse basis. Explicitly required fresh executions and current-state checks override reuse. A cache hit or execution label is not an actual run or proof of sufficiency.

For a record containing multiple checks, restrict record-level applicability when a changed check defeats reliance and identify affected scope. Inspect individual results and subjects instead of treating record-level current as universal success. Test success does not replace independent review; a fresh review does not refresh stale tests.

## Final closeout

Apply this section when planning, routing, reviewing or verifying governed completion. A standalone advisory assessment or scoped validation keeps its declared limits.

1. Complete every in-scope implementation milestone and required correction.
2. Obtain a fresh independent final whole-change Code Review of the complete delivered engineering change and cross-milestone interactions against current approved Design, Delivery allocation and relevant proof. Earlier milestone judgments inform but never substitute; even a single milestone or documentation-only change requires this assessment. The same nonauthor reviewer may perform a fresh final assessment. Corrections following final review require affected reassessment and an adequate integrated final judgment.
3. Verify separately assesses the entire required proposal/design/delivery/implementation/review/correction/proof chain, current concerns, authoritative and generated surfaces, and evidence adequacy. It cannot substitute its inspection for missing Code Review or approve its own correction. Only success produces the final explanation and completion evidence; failure records failed evidence and an owned blocker, not a success report.

The delivery plan names step 2 as a lifecycle-closeout checkpoint after implementation/corrections and before successful Verify. Delivery Review checks omission or milestone substitution as a plan defect. Route preserves this dependency. Integrated tests or a test-group non-applicability rationale cannot waive review.

PR, push, merge, release, publication and destructive authority remain separately owned. A saved review or Verify result supplies none of those permissions. A bounded CI repair may retain applicable judgments only with affirmative unchanged-basis evidence; substantive or ambiguous changes return to their owning activity and assessment.
