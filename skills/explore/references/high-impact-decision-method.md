# High-impact decision method

Load this method only for strategically broad or difficult-to-reverse decisions.

Identify stakeholders who receive value, bear migration or operational cost, approve irreversible effects, or support failure recovery. Compare staged and reversible forms of each direction, the point at which commitment becomes expensive, and the credible exit path.

Examine asymmetric failure: what happens if adoption is lower than expected, the key assumption is false, the change succeeds at greater scale than expected, or rollback occurs after partial adoption. Record which unanswered facts require bounded Research.

Recommend a leading direction only when the comparison supports it. Otherwise recommend a reversible experiment, a bounded research question, or explicit deferral to the decision owner.
