# Verify and validate an engineering slice

## Goal and inputs

Assess a bounded engineering outcome while keeping specified conformance and intended use distinguishable.
Select the applicable requirements, governed Scenarios, design, implementation/configuration and stakeholder context.

## Procedure

1. State the slice and the two claim scopes: which specified criteria apply, and whose intended outcome matters.
2. Use [verification planning](rem-methods-plan-and-assess-verification.md) to identify methods and observations, including integration failures that component checks could miss.
3. Use [intended-use planning](rem-methods-validate-stakeholder-outcomes.md) to establish representative conditions and outcome criteria. Record where a prototype or simulation differs from intended use.
4. Perform authorized assessments when their prerequisites exist. One activity may supply both kinds of evidence, but retain a separate conclusion for each question.
5. Inspect contradictory results, missing observations and stale evidence. A passing conformance check cannot settle an unobserved stakeholder outcome, and a successful demonstration cannot waive a failed specified obligation.
6. Return corrections to the responsible owner; repeat affected assessments and preserve the original meaning of earlier results.

## Outputs and limits

Produce two scoped conclusions with their evidence, applicability and remaining obligations, including an explicit unassessed result when work was only planned.
Project review and acceptance policy determine readiness; this Practice creates no extra lifecycle gate.
The [worked example](rem-practices-engineer-change-WORKED-EXAMPLE.md#assessment-plans) shows both plans without claiming execution.
