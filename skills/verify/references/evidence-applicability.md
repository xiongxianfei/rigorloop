# Final evidence applicability

Use for the exact final Verify assessment. Apply the packaged review-reliance policy and the governing plan's evidence requirements; this resource does not own judgment meaning or freshness policy.

For each planned or newly relevant obligation, identify the evidence, exact proved subjects, result, environment, limitations and decision to reuse, rerun or obtain new proof. Explain why reused evidence still protects the final subject. A materially changed surface, conflicting result or insufficient basis requires investigation and appropriate new proof. A missing delivery allocation returns to plan ownership.

Run or directly observe required checks and record exact commands and observed results. A command name, cache entry or author assertion alone is not execution evidence. Apply contract-required fresh checks. Unaffected evidence may be reused with justified applicability; do not invent a mandatory rerun of every command.

Reassess after corrections. Check exact final review scope and independence, current engineering subjects, required correction dispositions, unresolved blockers and evidence coherence before successful closeout. Recording bookkeeping alone is not an engineering change; relevant new evidence still affects reliance.
