# Successful final explanation

Use only after final Verify can justify success for its exact subject. Failed, inconclusive, interrupted or stale assessments remain recordable as evidence and concerns, but do not produce a successful final explanation or completion claim.

Explain what changed, why, how it realizes the approved Design and plan, important choices, supporting validation, limitations and residual risks. Bind the explanation to exact engineering subjects, the fresh independent whole-change Code Review and current evidence. Do not introduce requirements or repair upstream content.

Use the parent skill's targeted v2 `verify record` procedure with explicit applicability. Keep substantive success, durable save and current reliance distinct. Recording failure grants no durable completion. Read back the saved result and verify its exact basis. A changed basis requires reassessment; never retarget the old result.
