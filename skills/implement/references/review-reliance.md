# Assessment reliance and closeout

Before relying on a conclusion, inspect its scope, governing basis, actual reviewer/verifier, unresolved concerns and relevant changed support. Attribution is not authentication. File identities support bounded comparison; they do not prove independence, adequacy or whole-repository stability. Reported, compared and unknown applicability remain distinct.

A review retains the meaning of its actual assessment. Changed prepared input does not silently rewrite that assessment or approve new subjects. Material corrections return to their author, followed by proportionate independent reassessment and adequate coverage of the complete current scope. A responsible assessor can explicitly justify harmless continuation; uncertain effects remain needs-reassessment. Bookkeeping alone does not automatically restart review, but contradictory evidence cannot be ignored.

Open findings survive omission and later clean judgments until explicit disposition. Deferral needs an accountable follow-up and cannot waive mandatory acceptance. Preserve only detail still needed by current decisions. Comparable working evidence may be replaced; preserve the assessment-owned support still needed to explain its conclusion. An accepted Basis whose approval later loses support retains the actor's decision and exposes a current reliance gap.

One mandatory whole-change Code Review gate follows implementation. Interim advisory review is optional. Milestones are implementation/check checkpoints, not approval gates. A single milestone still has exactly one whole-change gate; corrections may require multiple attempts within it. Final Verify separately assesses whether the current evidence and completed work support the completion claim.

Reuse unaffected evidence with explicit scope and applicability. New review or a command retry alone does not require every test to rerun; changed subjects, procedure, environment or contrary evidence can require renewed proof. A later current Review does not renew stale Verification support automatically. Final success requires currently supported accepted bases, reviewed delivery intent, whole-change approval, relevant evidence and dispositioned work/issues.

Only successful final Verify supports compact historical completion. After completion, the Change stops tracking current system applicability. Later regressions become linked new Changes; corrections to the original account are explicit notes. PR, push, merge, release and publication require their own authority, which may already be supplied by the user. Recording or installation grants none of it.
