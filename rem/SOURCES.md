# REM source references

Sources support the particular claims below; they do not establish REM as a whole or prescribe its local cardinalities, 5W2H policy, workflow or representation.
The method remains understandable from its canonical documents without fetching these publications. References inherit the [canonical method edition and KPS basis](metadata.md); source identifiers below identify particular supported claims, not separate publications.
