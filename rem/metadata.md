# REM method metadata

This is the canonical declaration of the REM edition and the KPS edition on which it is based.

```yaml
name: REM
version: "1.0.0"
based_on:
  name: KPS
  version: "9.0.0"
```

## Meaning and scope

The REM version identifies this edition of the engineering method. The `based_on` relationship records the selected knowledge framework and its exact version. It does not, by itself, establish assessed KPS conformance, empirical effectiveness, a software package version or release publication.

Maintained REM knowledge documents inherit this declaration. Their stable identities identify individual Concepts, Principles, Models, Methods and Practices; they do not need independently maintained copies of the method version or KPS basis. A future change to either value is made here and reconciled with affected guidance.

Publication years, source editions, access dates and inspection provenance describe the referenced publications and actual assessment activities. They remain with their individual reference notes and are not replaced by the REM version. Imported document metadata is input for reconciliation and cannot override this declaration or establish an inspection merely by being copied.

Current REM Models and Methods retain authority for engineering rules. Declaring a KPS basis does not suspend those rules or automatically replace an approved project contract.
