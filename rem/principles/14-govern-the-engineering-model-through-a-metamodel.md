# Principle 14: Govern the engineering model through a metamodel

## Relationship and why

A model is interpretable only when its entity types and relationships have a shared meaning. Rules about those types, permitted relationships and their evolution form a model of the engineering model itself. Making that metamodel explicit gives authors and validators a common basis for detecting incompatible interpretations, while leaving engineering adequacy to assessment.

## REM commitment

Operational Support defines valid structures, relationships, lifecycle, and maintenance rules.

## Application

[Operational Support](../models/operational-support.md) owns the metamodel and its representation, validation and maintenance rules.
