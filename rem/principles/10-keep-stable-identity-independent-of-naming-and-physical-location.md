# Principle 10: Keep stable identity independent of naming and physical location

## Relationship and why

Names describe meaning and locations help people find an entity, but both can change while the entity continues to represent the same engineering thing. Keeping identity separate preserves the connection between references and their subject through a rename or move; current references still need reconciliation.

## REM commitment

Renaming or moving an entity does not itself create a new identity.

## Application

[Operational Support](../models/operational-support.md) owns representation and maintenance conventions; the [representation model](../models/README.md#representation) preserves identity independently of those choices.
