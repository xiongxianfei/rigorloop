# Principle 11: Let Changes evolve Baselines without replacing current definitions

## Relationship and why

A current definition answers what applies now; a Change explains a transition and a Baseline identifies a state. Keeping these roles distinct lets engineers understand the present directly while retaining the history needed to explain how it arose. Otherwise, interpreting current meaning can depend on reconstructing a long sequence of past edits.

## REM commitment

Current assets remain understandable without replaying the complete Change history.

## Application

The [Evolution model](../models/README.md#evolution) relates Changes, Baselines and current definitions; [Engineer a change](../practices/engineer-change/README.md) applies that relationship.
