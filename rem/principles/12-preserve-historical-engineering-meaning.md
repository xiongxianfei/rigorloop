# Principle 12: Preserve historical engineering meaning

## Relationship and why

An earlier decision or assessment refers to the meanings, responsibilities and conditions of its own state. Substituting current names or allocations into that account can make it appear to support something it never assessed. Preserving the original meaning keeps historical explanations and later comparisons attributable to the right state.

## REM commitment

Later allocations, names, and decisions must not rewrite what an earlier state meant.

## Application

The [Evolution model](../models/README.md#evolution) and [realization history](../models/architecture-realization.md#realization-and-history) preserve earlier responsibility and realization meaning.
