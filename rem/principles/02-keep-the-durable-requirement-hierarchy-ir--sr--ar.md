# Principle 2: Keep the durable requirement hierarchy IR → SR → AR

## Relationship and why

A stakeholder need, a system obligation and an obligation assigned within an architecture answer different questions. Relating those levels keeps local engineering decisions connected to their originating need while making each level assessable in its own context. REM selects the IR → SR → AR hierarchy to express that relationship; the chosen structure is a method rule, not a claim that every engineering method must use the same hierarchy.

## REM commitment

Initial needs, system obligations, and allocated obligations have distinct meanings and containment levels.
