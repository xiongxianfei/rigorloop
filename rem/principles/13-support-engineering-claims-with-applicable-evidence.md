# Principle 13: Support engineering claims with applicable evidence

## Relationship and why

Observations support a claim through their relevance to its subject, criteria and conditions. Evidence collected for one situation does not automatically justify a conclusion about another, and an assessment plan does not establish that its expected observations occurred. Making those relationships explicit helps engineers judge the reach and limits of a conclusion.

## REM commitment

Distinguish the intended assessment, the observations actually obtained, and the judgment they support.

## Application

The [Assurance model](../models/README.md#assurance) relates assessments, evidence and judgments; [verification](../methods/plan-and-assess-verification.md) and [intended-use validation](../methods/validate-stakeholder-outcomes.md) apply that distinction to their respective claims.
