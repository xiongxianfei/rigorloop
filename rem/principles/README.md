# REM principles

A Principle answers: **what deeper explanatory relationship matters, and why?** These eleven principles explain relationships, mechanisms, scope and limits. Their examples are illustrative, not executed evidence. They do not claim universal empirical validity or uniquely determine REM's chosen rules.

[Concepts](../concepts/README.md) define terms. [Models](../models/README.md) own selected structures and invariants; [Methods](../methods/README.md) own procedures; [Operational Support](../models/operational-support.md#clear-engineering-definitions) owns shared authoring rules. Each principle links to those current owners and scoped [source references](../SOURCES.md).

The eleven named identities are new explanatory identities; they do not renumber the old commitments. Existing requirement, Scenario, allocation, authority, evidence and view-tailoring rules remain applicable.
