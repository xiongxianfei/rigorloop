# Principle 8: Use trees for true containment and typed graphs across domains

## Relationship and why

Containment answers which whole an element belongs to; other relationships answer questions such as what it realizes, constrains or interacts with. Those connections can cross containment boundaries. Distinguishing the relationship types preserves their meaning without forcing a dependency or contribution to masquerade as parentage.

## REM commitment

Do not force the entire engineering system into one hierarchy.

## Application

The [model composition and cardinalities](../models/README.md) distinguish requirement and Module containment from typed cross-domain relationships.
