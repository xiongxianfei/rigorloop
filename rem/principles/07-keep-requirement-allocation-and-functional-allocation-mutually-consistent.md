# Principle 7: Keep requirement allocation and functional allocation mutually consistent

## Relationship and why

An obligation assigned to one responsibility can depend on behavior allocated elsewhere. If those assignments are considered separately, a boundary or required collaboration can be overlooked. Comparing them exposes whether the responsible parts and their interactions can jointly satisfy the obligation.

## REM commitment

Architectural responsibility must reconcile the behavior performed with the obligations it must satisfy.

## Application

[Allocation consistency](../models/architecture-allocation.md) owns the relationships; [Architecture Allocation](../methods/architecture-allocation.md) explains how to resolve responsibility and interaction gaps.
