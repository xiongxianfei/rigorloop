# Principle 1: Separate requirements from system assets

## Relationship and why

Required outcomes and the designs that satisfy them can change for different reasons. A design can change while its underlying obligation remains valid; keeping the two distinct lets engineers evaluate alternatives without losing the need against which a solution is judged.

## REM commitment

Requirements express what must become or remain true; assets describe capabilities, behavior, and architecture.

## Application

[Requirements](../models/requirements.md) distinguish obligations from the [Features and Functions](../models/system-design.md) and [architecture assets](../models/architecture-design.md) used to satisfy them.
