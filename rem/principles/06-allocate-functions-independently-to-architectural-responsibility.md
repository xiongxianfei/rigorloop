# Principle 6: Allocate Functions independently to architectural responsibility

## Relationship and why

Behavior describes what the system does, while an obligation states what must hold. Those perspectives overlap without being interchangeable: assigning a Function does not by itself account for every condition imposed on its realization. Keeping the two allocation paths explicit makes their agreement something engineers can check.

## REM commitment

Logical behavior and requirement obligations approach architecture from different directions.

## Application

[Architecture allocation](../models/architecture-allocation.md) defines Function and AR allocation separately; [Architecture Allocation](../methods/architecture-allocation.md) reconciles them.
