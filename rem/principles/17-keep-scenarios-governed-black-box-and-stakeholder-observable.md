# Principle 17: Keep Scenarios governed, black-box, and stakeholder-observable

## Relationship and why

A stakeholder situation can remain valid across different internal designs. Describing it through externally observable conditions and outcomes preserves that independence, allowing the same situation to inform requirements and assess alternative realizations. Stable Scenario identity and lifecycle make its continuing meaning identifiable as the engineering model evolves.

## REM commitment

Scenarios preserve concrete stakeholder situations with stable identity and lifecycle while avoiding internal Functions, Modules, Interfaces, or implementation sequences; they inform SR analysis rather than replacing system design.

## Application

The [Scenario model](../models/scenarios.md) owns identity, lifecycle, ownership and black-box boundaries; [Scenario Analysis](../methods/scenario-analysis.md) develops stakeholder situations without substituting internal design.
