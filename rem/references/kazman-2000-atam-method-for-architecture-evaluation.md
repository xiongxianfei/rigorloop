# Kazman Klein and Clements ATAM Method for Architecture Evaluation

## Key takeaway

Architectural choices can affect several quality goals, so their tradeoffs need explicit analysis.

## Summary

The inspected abstract motivates architectural analysis of competing quality concerns. REM uses that bounded contribution in its decision Practice. Detailed sensitivity and scenario locators from the incoming document are retained below as reported reading leads; REM’s procedure is not a full ATAM evaluation.

## Independence and limitations
Institutional method-development work with practice experience, not a head-to-head experiment establishing that the REM procedure improves outcomes. It is distinct from NASA guidance. Common engineering ideas do not guarantee completely independent intellectual origins.

## What it does not establish
REM’s comparison sheet is not certified ATAM. A weighted total is not proof that an option is best. This record supplies no modern cryptographic recommendation: historical examples are not reused as current security advice.
