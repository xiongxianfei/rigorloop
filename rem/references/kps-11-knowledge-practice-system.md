# Knowledge Practice System Core 11.0.0

## Key takeaway

KPS supplies authoring guidance for the proposed REM coverage outline; consulting a newer Core does not change REM's selected basis.

## Summary

The inspected KPS source distinguishes its content release, authoring language, checker revision and Core-specific curation policy.
Its guidance connects required subject understanding with practical capability and source-faithful local explanations.
REM uses these ideas as editorial input to a draft; current engineering rules and the [declared method basis](../metadata.md) retain their own authority.
