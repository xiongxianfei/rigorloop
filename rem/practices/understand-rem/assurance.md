## 6. Verification, Evidence, and Engineering Claims

The assurance knowledge is built from the [Verification, Evidence, Judgment, and Traceability concepts](../../concepts/assurance.md#assurance-and-traceability), the [evidence applicability Principle](../../principles/evidence-applies-to-a-claim-and-context.md), and the [Assurance model](../../models/README.md#assurance).

The core relationship is:

```text
Requirement
    │ verifiedBy
    ▼
Verification
    │ produces
    ▼
Evidence
    │ supports
    ▼
Judgment
```

REM keeps these concepts separate because:

- a Requirement states what must be true;
- Verification defines how it will be assessed;
- Evidence records what was actually observed;
- Judgment states what the applicable Evidence supports.

The [Verification method](../../methods/plan-and-assess-verification.md) applies these concepts and relationships to planned checks, actual observations and scoped conclusions.
The [intended-use validation method](../../methods/validate-stakeholder-outcomes.md) separately assesses stakeholder outcomes in representative conditions.
Both methods preserve the distinction between planned assessment, actual observations and a justified conclusion.
