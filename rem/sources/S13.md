# S13 — Gotel and Finkelstein — An Analysis of the Requirements Traceability Problem

## Reference

[Gotel and Finkelstein — An Analysis of the Requirements Traceability Problem](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/749/1/2.2_rtprob.pdf)

## Reading locator

1994 International Conference on Requirements Engineering, pp. 94–101; §5.2 and Figure 1 in the institutional PDF.

## Use and limits

Distinguishes pre-specification traceability of requirement formation from post-specification traceability of subsequent development. REM applies the distinction to origin and realization; its edge types and retention choices are local decisions.

## Provenance

Primary source inspected on 2026-10-07 within the scope stated above. This note supports explanatory reasoning, not approval or empirical validation of REM. Examples in the principles are authored illustrations.
