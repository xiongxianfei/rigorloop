# S12 — Zave and Jackson — Four Dark Corners of Requirements Engineering

## Reference

[Zave and Jackson — Four Dark Corners of Requirements Engineering](https://www.pamelazave.com/4dc.pdf)

## Reading locator

Author-hosted manuscript; §5 (especially environment constraints and domain assumptions); journal publication ACM TOSEM 6(1), 1997, pp. 1–30, DOI 10.1145/237432.237434. The inspected manuscript is 35 PDF pages, not the journal pagination.

## Use and limits

Specifications and domain knowledge jointly support requirement satisfaction. Some assumptions are probable rather than invariant. This supplies a conditional argument, not empirical confirmation that a particular environment satisfies its assumptions or a prescription of REM cardinalities.

## Provenance

Primary source inspected on 2026-10-07 within the scope stated above. This note supports explanatory reasoning, not approval or empirical validation of REM. Examples in the principles are authored illustrations.
