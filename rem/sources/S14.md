## Reference

[Kazman, Klein and Clements — ATAM: Method for Architecture Evaluation](https://www.sei.cmu.edu/library/atam-method-for-architecture-evaluation/)

## Reading locator

CMU/SEI-2000-TR-004, 2000; official report-page abstract.

## Use and limits

Architectural analysis examines tradeoffs among quality concerns such as performance, availability, security and modifiability. Only the official abstract is relied on here; this is not a full ATAM procedure, a validated ranking of options or evidence for a particular scoring scheme.

## Provenance

Primary source inspected on 2026-10-07 within the scope stated above. This note supports explanatory reasoning, not approval or empirical validation of REM. Examples in the principles are authored illustrations.
