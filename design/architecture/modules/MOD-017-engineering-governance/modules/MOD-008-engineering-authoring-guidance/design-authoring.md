## Authoring responsibilities

| Skill | Owns | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| requirement-analysis | RR interpretation; reuse/refinement/creation of IR/SR; supporting Feature and black-box Scenario analysis | Architectural allocation, implementation tasks or its own approval |
| system-design | Functions, logical behavior and relationships to Features/SRs | New stakeholder intent or physical deployment by inference |
| architecture-design | Accountable Modules, Interfaces, AR derivation/allocation, state/data authority, material realization and views | Requirement acceptance, delivery task completion or review approval |
| plan | Delivery milestones, dependencies, proof allocation and recovery against the accepted basis | Missing requirements, Module responsibilities or ARs |

Use the owning [Requirements model](../../../../../../rem/models/requirements.md), [System Design model](../../../../../../rem/models/system-design.md) and [Architecture Design model](../../../../../../rem/models/architecture-design.md). One request can affect several existing owners. Reuse without modification is a valid result; do not create one entity, Function, Module or task for every request/requirement merely for symmetry.

## Living model and views

Edit the authoritative owner of each fact and reconcile its affected references, examples and consumers. Preserve stable IDs when responsibility is unchanged. Names, directories and technology selections do not by themselves create new logical Modules. Canonical applicable rationale belongs with its design owner; a decision record may explain the work but must not become the only place to learn the current system.

A model's views are projections or focused explanations of its definition. They do not become separate authoritative copies. Use [Architecture Views](../../../../../../rem/methods/architecture-views.md) to select useful Logical, Process, Development, Physical and Scenario explanations. The Scenario model stays black-box; internal participants belong in its architectural Scenario View. Technical realization can explain a logical responsibility; deployed instances and hosts belong in Physical placement. Development explains source/package organization and dependencies at the level useful for ownership, without requiring unstable individual filenames everywhere.

Generate data-driven views from repository data and author only the additional explanations needed to understand behavior or decisions. Prefer the project's supported D2 diagrams for authored dependency, sequence and state explanations. A sequence diagram is useful when ordered interactions matter; use clear descriptive alternatives/optional paths without making unexplained notation a prerequisite. Reuse the existing browser and its source model data. Zero, one and several graphs per view are valid; graph navigation must not require a duplicate website.

## Proportionate design and proof

A small refinement may be a few lines in an existing model. Do not create a standalone proposal, RR file, responsibility or review dossier solely to satisfy a document sequence. Capture unresolved questions and material decisions where their owner can resolve them. Review input identifies exact affected definitions, the reason for change, reused basis, interactions and material uncertainty.

Include observable success, failure and relevant quality constraints. Apply [shared test-design rules](../../../../../support/test-design/rules.md) and the owning model's coverage intent. Design specifies what must be demonstrated; planning allocates concrete execution and proof; implementation supplies actual checks. Retire obsolete exclusive fixtures and preserve required negative/regression protection when behavior changes. Do not mistake a schema pass or a rendered graph for engineering verification.

Templates under `templates/` support repository authoring; published skills carry their selected resources and do not depend on this repository's maintainer layout. Packaged REM references are generated, versioned copies of the canonical sources, not separately maintained methodology.

## Reconciliation and history

Move an applicable obligation to its current owner or record an explicit retirement disposition before deleting a former source. Preserve required historical originals and original assessment meaning. New source structure does not renew an earlier approval. The prior combined `design` skill and its document-pilot conventions are recoverable at commit `39be9c81`, under `skills/design/` and `docs/design/skill/authoring/design.md`. They are replaced by the responsibility split above, not retained as another active authoring route.

### Boundary scan and acceptance scenarios

| Dimension | Requirement basis | Distinct outcome to demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input domain | DES-SR-01 | Unknown contracts, malformed references or unsupported scope stop the affected operation without inferred defaults. |
| State/lifecycle | DES-SR-01 | Progress, accepted basis, review judgment, final Verify and historical completion remain distinct; saved state alone advances none. |
| Identity/authority | DES-SR-01 | The actual responsible actor, declared scope and current support govern reliance; an identifier or role label does not establish authority. |
| Composition/path | DES-SR-01 | Changed producer and consumer contracts are reconciled together, including packaged conditional resources and referenced engineering definitions. |
| Temporal/retry | DES-SR-01 | A changed basis requires rereading and proportionate reassessment; an old submission does not acquire current authority on retry. |
| Failure/recovery | DES-SR-01 | Interrupted work exposes its actual outcome and an owned next step without erasing unresolved issues or inventing success. |
| Compatibility/migration | DES-SR-01 | Retired procedures remain historical; successor behavior requires explicit applicable adoption/import and cannot relabel old approval. |
| External/environment | DES-SR-01 | Local engineering results remain separate from installed, published or hosted outcomes; required observations must actually be made. |

## Test design

Inspect the current responsibilities and boundary scenarios against the owning REM model and Module contract. Structural checks establish format only; independent review judges semantic coverage. Runtime record behavior is exercised by the package’s operational store, update, reliance, review and maintenance tests; skill guidance is assessed in actual generated archives with the resource validator and independent scenario inspection. Required combined and negative proof is allocated in the adoption plan.
