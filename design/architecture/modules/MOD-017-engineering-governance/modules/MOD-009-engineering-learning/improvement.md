# Supported lessons and observed improvement

[MOD-009](module.json) owns FUNC-035–038 and AR-082–085 under SR-035–038. The [existing Learning contract](learning.md) retains public Learn operations, contributor confirmation, session/topic custody and bounded route-result updates. This document defines the IR-007 cooperation and realization around those responsibilities. It introduces neither a Learn session nor a requirement to produce a lesson for every correction.

## Responsibility and content authority

MOD-017 provides [IF-017](../../../../interfaces/IF-017-engineering-lesson-and-improvement-preparation/interface.json) to MOD-012 for lesson characterization, context-based selection, improvement proposals and observed-effect comparison. MOD-009 contributes the learning meaning and owns the working context. MOD-017 owns this external contract consistently with its existing guidance and assurance contracts; child Function/AR allocation is not duplicated on the parent.

An accountable participant supplies observations, causal reasoning, applicability and proposed conclusions. IF-017 prepares and explains those supplied meanings; it is not an automatic causal assessor, evidence search engine, policy writer or execution service. MOD-007 retains generic evidence applicability, criterion coverage and formal judgments through IF-010/014. MOD-006 retains action authority and controlled transitions through IF-009/016. MOD-012 carries exact participant-selected content between these contracts and the selected writer; there is no hidden MOD-009-to-store or MOD-009-to-destination-owner call.

A lesson uses the selected project's existing documentary representation, including the current Learn session/topic form when that operation is selected. Its inspectable content distinguishes a local identity/locator and exact content basis, finding/source and original context, observed facts, inferred mechanism, support and contrary information, reuse rationale, applicable situations/exclusions, accountable author and remaining uncertainty. The related proposal adds affected authoritative subjects and identities, owner, intended behavior and observable success/adverse-effect criteria. These are semantic content requirements, not a new mandatory REM entity, JSON schema or public command.

Keep lesson content, proposal, governed disposition, actual adopted version and effectiveness observations separately identifiable. A material revision gets a new content basis while retaining the association to its prior meaning when still relied upon. Stable topic identity or a mutable file path alone cannot retarget an old observation or adoption decision. Redacted or unavailable evidence remains explicitly limited; do not expose private source material or claim that a missing source was inspected.

## Supported lesson characterization

Start from an identifiable finding and attributable observations in its original activity and version. The participant explains which facts are observed and which causal steps are inferred, including alternative explanations and contrary observations material to the claim. A plausible cause does not become a fact because it has been written into a topic. Missing or contradictory support leaves the causal conclusion provisional and identifies the missing assessment; the original supported observations may still be retained.

Apply the declared reuse conditions: a recurring failure, a reusable weakness in guidance or controls, or a single high-consequence event with supported broader applicability. State the mechanism and the contexts in which that reasoning is useful, including counterconditions. Recurrence alone does not prove a cause, and a one-off event needs a demonstrated reusable implication rather than an invented recurrence count. A finding with no supported broader implication stays a local correction with its owning work, with that disposition explicit.

For selected Learn operations, [learning.md](learning.md#specialist-contract) still requires contributor confirmation before dependent topic or route effects. An analytical draft or supported characterization is not that confirmation. Pending or rejected classification permits retention of its declared session disposition, not topic or route mutation. An already applicable grant is respected within its actual scope; IF-017 neither invents a new approval gate nor bypasses the selected operation's confirmation boundary.

## Lesson selection for planned work

The participant supplies the planned activity, relevant project/profile/version and material conditions, and an explicitly selected available lesson population. Acquire each candidate from its current accountable source with exact inspected content identity and known availability; an author-selected list or local topic scope is sufficient when its limits are stated. There is no claim to have discovered all possible lessons, polled external owners or searched private history automatically.

For each candidate, compare the stated activity/context against recorded conditions, mechanism and exclusions. Present applicable, inapplicable or unresolved reasoning with the source basis. Name material differences rather than presenting the lesson as an unconditional instruction. A known exclusion differs from insufficient context; an unavailable source differs from a completely inspected available scope with no match. A partial acquisition cannot produce an unqualified no-match result. Derived indexes are disposable candidate aids and cannot silently substitute for current topic/source content.

Present associated improvement disposition from the selected authoritative change decision and exact affected version, including unknown, proposed, rejected or revision-required scope. Confirm the decision's subject association and current applicability; a copied status label or finished route is insufficient. An adopted rule is linked to its current authoritative owner and actual adopted scope, not copied into a competing lesson policy. If disposition is unavailable, the lesson's supported observations remain readable, but no adopted-current-rule claim is supplied.

Changed sources or activity context during preparation require a new consistent selection or an explicit mixed/unresolved basis. The captured result states what was inspected and does not claim continuous live currentness. Consultation remains read-only; it grants no authority to modify a model, perform an improvement or change project policy.

## Accountable proposal and controlled adoption

A proposal identifies the supporting lesson/mechanism and source basis, applicable situations, exact current affected guidance/checks/artifacts, accountable destination owner, intended changed behavior, and independently observable success and adverse-effect criteria. Criteria precede adoption and assessment; copying a desired success label or the proposed answer is not an assessable expectation. Missing owner, subject, support or criterion prevents a ready-for-adoption claim while allowing a clearly incomplete proposal to be retained.

MOD-012 gives the proposal to its actual artifact/decision owner through the authorized workflow. IF-009 interprets applicable existing authority; IF-016 prepares controlled transition meaning from supplied decisions, baseline descriptors and observed effects. The decision owner can accept, require revision or reject the exact proposal under the selected project policy. Neither a recommendation nor IF-017 preparation invokes the owner, decides approval or applies a change. If another owner must act, retain a bounded handoff with that responsibility and its unresolved work.

Adoption association identifies the exact lesson/proposal basis, governing decision, affected before/result state or baseline, and the owner-produced result needed to establish the claimed adopted scope. An approved direction or completed scheduling route alone does not establish changed artifact contents. Report authorized-but-unperformed or uncertain effects explicitly. A policy adoption decision can establish only the scope it actually governs; installed tools or changed model bytes require their own actual outcomes. Rejection and requested revision preserve the original finding and proposal meaning.

Under the existing Learn contract, a `durable-scheduled-follow-up` may settle its route while the artifact change remains pending. An `authoritative-artifact` route needs its qualifying exact owner result. `record-learn-route-result` continues to update only the selected backlink, settlement and blocker; it cannot poll/invoke the owner, rewrite classification or make adoption/effectiveness claims. Route settlement, controlled adoption and effectiveness are separate axes.

## Opportunity-based effect assessment

The improvement owner prepares the assessment using the adopted version, lesson mechanism, applicability and success/adverse-effect criteria. Bind the method to exact expected content, participant/observer, activity context and relevant conditions before judging the observations. Changing the criteria after seeing an outcome creates a new assessment basis and cannot rewrite an earlier failure into success.

First distinguish the triggering situation from later engineering use. When reproducible, an authorized participant performs the reproduction against the adopted version and supplies its actual correction outcome. When not reproducible, record why and the unobserved scope; a proposed reproduction or passing unrelated check is not a result. Reproduction is useful correction evidence but cannot substitute for the next comparable activity.

At work preparation or a later reported opportunity, compare the actual activity with the recorded mechanism, version, task and relevant conditions. The responsible participant supplies that comparability reasoning, the available activity scope and any unknown gaps. Within that declared known scope, identify the next comparable activity after adoption. Do not quietly choose a later successful activity while omitting an earlier comparable failure. If completeness or ordering cannot be established, limit the statement to the actually observed opportunity and leave the claimed next-opportunity coverage unresolved. No background monitor, scheduler or automatic chronology is introduced.

For that activity, record whether the mechanism recurred, the intended changed behavior occurred, and new confusion, burden or other defined adverse effects appeared. Preserve the actual task/output, observed adopted version, applicable criterion basis, source/observer and support/contradictions for each conclusion. An absent report is not an observed absence of recurrence. Incomplete or conflicting observations remain visible and restrict the claim; a mixed outcome cannot be silently summarized as successful improvement.

If no comparable activity occurred, effectiveness remains unassessed even when adoption or triggering reproduction succeeded. If comparable work occurred but required observations are absent, identify the missing evidence rather than saying no opportunity occurred. Conclusions apply only to observed activities and stated conditions, with no universal duration, sample threshold or guarantee against recurrence.

On recurrence or a material change to mechanism, guidance, adopted version, work context or criteria, identify which previous conclusion is affected and why current reliance needs reassessment. Preserve its original observations and bounded meaning. A new comparable opportunity and accountable assessment may support a new conclusion; merely planning it supplies no observations. MOD-007's IF-010/014 preserves generic applicability and judgment rules, including contrary evidence and required independence. MOD-009 supplies domain-specific comparability/effect reasoning without giving its own preparation formal approval.

## Retention and failure handling

For the existing Learn session/topic path, apply learning.md's no-clobber, contributor-confirmation and identity-bound idempotence. The selected local writer must establish actual exclusive custody over all relevant session/topic/route writers before its final checks, and hold that custody through mutation and readback. A project writer may supply an established exclusive workspace or an effective shared exclusion mechanism; a claimed single writer, an advisory marker ignored by other writers or an earlier inspection is insufficient. If the environment cannot establish this exclusion, withhold mutation and retain the prepared content and missing custody condition. This Design does not presume an installed lock service.

While holding custody, recheck the expected current content identities, unused creation target, contributor confirmation and exact route/result association. Only then perform the authorized effects and inspect the resulting bytes before releasing custody. A path collision selects a safe authorized unused target under the same guard; a changed topic or conflicting route result stops for reconciliation. Matching retries require the same semantic basis and confirmed result. After interruption, lost custody or a lost receipt, reacquire custody and inspect actual retained bytes before deciding whether any remaining action is safe. Retain complete, partial or unresolved effects separately from prepared intent; exclusive custody does not make several file writes a transaction or justify blind replay. Preparation does not prove a saved topic or completed destination work.

When governed operational recording is explicitly selected, MOD-012 composes supplied method/observation/judgment content through IF-014 and existing IF-004/003 tasks. Native fields and exact retained supporting attachments carry content lacking a native field; there is no native lesson or improvement entity added to the record schema. Selected reads confirm actual retention and recover the relied-on basis. An expected-revision conflict or changed engineering subject requires rereading and reassessment of the intended update, not blind replay. Retention success proves neither causal adequacy, adoption nor effectiveness.

Standalone use identifies the chosen artifact and responsible writer with its observed outcome. No selected working or retained mechanism means no saved-account claim. Historical sessions and judgments keep their original protocol/meaning; new guidance neither auto-imports them into v4 nor migrates a historical session into a route-result target. Related engineering definitions remain with their existing canonical owners; retention of current rationale follows IR-001, and retirement must preserve surviving reliance rather than deleting still-needed learning support.

## Realization and design rationale

The selected realization is participant-assisted local reasoning and documentary content. Existing `skills/learn/` sources and mapped learning resources own any later public procedural delivery; this Design does not edit or certify an installed skill. Local project session/topic artifacts remain subordinate guidance under the selected Learning contract. MOD-012 supplies invocation and explicit command composition; MOD-010/011 supply operational retention when selected. No new service, database, automated cause detector, notification mechanism or public API is selected.

MOD-017 composes the learning, authority and assurance responsibilities without acquiring their child state. The [owned Process diagrams](README.md#lesson-and-improvement-interactions) show the proposal/adoption boundary and later lesson use/effect assessment. The derived Logical view shows MOD-009 Functions/ARs and IF-017's parent provider and external consumer. Development ownership is the canonical learning contract and future skill resources; Physical placement is the participant workspace and explicitly selected artifact or operational store. Canonical Scenarios remain black-box.

Reusing IF-008 wholesale would conflate canonical authoring rules with supported lessons that may be provisional or rejected. IF-014 supplies generic assurance preparation but does not choose reusable mechanisms or comparable opportunities. IF-017 therefore exposes one cohesive learning contract, while adoption and generic judgment reuse their existing owners. Four ARs constrain the four distinct obligations at MOD-009; no second storage, authority or assurance owner is created merely to fill the requirement tree.

## Design inspection and acceptance intent

These cases are proposed semantic observations, not executed learning sessions or evidence that an improvement worked. Use a small synthetic topic, exact before/after guidance and two identified work activities; prepare expected classifications and effect criteria independently of the candidate being assessed. Each case keeps its own mutable working copy and preserves the relevant original observations.

| Basis and plausible violation | Prepared case and independent expected result |
| --- | --- |
| SR-035 / AR-082: an unsupported cause becomes durable policy | Contrast two observed recurrences with an unsupported causal explanation, a supported single high-consequence reusable weakness, and a local typo correction. Keep facts/inferences distinct, require an actual reuse rationale, return the typo to its owning work, and retain contrary evidence. Pending contributor confirmation has no topic/route effect. |
| SR-036 / AR-083: unavailable or excluded learning is mistaken for no match or current rule | Consult a selected topic population with an applicable lesson, an explicit exclusion, a missing source and an incomplete work context. | SR-037 / AR-084: a proposal or completed route is called adopted | Compare a complete proposal with missing-owner/subject/criterion variants; supply an accepted decision with no required artifact result, a rejected decision, a scheduled-follow-up route and a confirmed owner-produced adopted version. Only the last establishes the claimed changed artifact; all retain the lesson and governing decision basis. |
| SR-038 / AR-085: elapsed time or a reproduction pass becomes effectiveness | Contrast nonreproducibility, reproduction success with no later work, a comparable opportunity with missing observations, observed recurrence/new burden, and a later success after a skipped earlier comparable failure. Keep no-opportunity and missing-evidence distinct; require actual criteria and comparable context, preserve mixed results and do not claim the skipped occurrence was assessed. |
| Reassessment and custody | Change the adopted version or context after a bounded favorable result. Attempt competing local writes with unavailable exclusion, then interrupt a guarded topic write or lose a save receipt. Withhold unguarded mutation; under actual custody recheck identity, confirmation and route basis through effects/readback. Preserve the original conclusion and partial effects, expose reassessment/retention uncertainty and reacquire custody before inspecting a possible retry. |
| Direct IR-007 outcome | Follow a supported finding into a relevant lesson, an accountable controlled improvement and later comparable observations. Existing schema and browser checks protect structure and presentation only. Learning's existing semantic test-design groups remain applicable and separate from these composed IR-007 outcomes. Actual participant execution, improvement adoption and effectiveness require their own observations and applicable judgments.
