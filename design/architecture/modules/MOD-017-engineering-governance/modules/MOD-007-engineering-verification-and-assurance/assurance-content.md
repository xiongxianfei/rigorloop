# Assurance content and current reliance

MOD-007 owns FUNC-027–031 and AR-062–066 under IR-004. [Assessment](assessment.md) retains review policy and gate meanings; this contract defines the content and cooperation that make those policies usable. It specifies target Design, not installed automation, executed verification or requirement satisfaction.

## Boundaries and authority

An assessor supplies the engineering meaning: intended technique, actual observations, applicability rationale, contradiction dispositions and judgment. MOD-007 prepares and inspects that content without impersonating the assessor. MOD-017 provides the consumer contract [IF-014](../../../../interfaces/IF-014-assurance-content-preparation/interface.json) to MOD-012's guided assessment activity. IF-010 remains the read-only claim-support analysis; IF-014 adds preparation of attributable content, not another assessment authority. MOD-006 retains progression and applicable action authority; MOD-008 retains general authoring methods.

MOD-012 arranges the actor's explicit recording through IF-004. MOD-018 composes MOD-010 command admission with MOD-011 retention through IF-003. MOD-007 does not access a database or treat an interface return as retention. This participant-mediated composition avoids an undeclared direct MOD-007 to MOD-011 call. Standalone human assessment uses the same content meanings without requiring CLI adoption; its selected artifact owner must confirm actual retention before reporting saved content.

Execution of the verification technique belongs to the selected human, agent or external tool under its existing authority. No universal test runner, scheduler, automatic tool invocation or permission is introduced. A technique may be an inspection or a measured experiment. MOD-007 accepts attributable reports and exposes missing provenance; it cannot independently authenticate a human assertion or discover every omitted contrary observation.

## Common content and admission

Every prepared item identifies its kind, stable local identity, author or accountable source, selected project/Change or standalone scope, exact subject/state basis and applicable criterion basis. A criterion reference includes requirement identity, the criterion's exact content/revision and its position or explicit stable identifier; a number alone is not a binding across reordered criteria. A subject records the observed identity and inspection scope, distinguishing present, absent, inaccessible and not inspected. Conditions identify the relevant environment and assumptions, including unknowns. Capture is explicit; current filesystem labels or timestamps are not sufficient state identity.

The logical content kinds are method definition, occurrence/observation, applicability assessment, criterion coverage and engineering judgment. These describe distinct content, not a new public command grammar or persistent schema. An unsupported kind or ambiguous identity returns a rejected preparation with diagnostics. A semantically incomplete but identifiable item returns its original supplied content, named missing fields and affected scope. It can be retained as incomplete information where the selected record contract allows it; preparation cannot coerce it into a complete definition or favorable judgment.

Preparation takes a coherent selected record read and the assessor's explicitly inspected engineering bases. Record revision, engineering subject identities and content identity remain separate. External subject files are not locked by an operational read: preserve the observed basis and identify concurrent or uninspected changes. Return the exact prepared content and its basis, identified gaps, restrictions on reliance and remaining actor decisions. No automatic modification of a supplied conclusion, implicit narrowed claim or invented observation is permitted. A proposed unsupported favorable assertion may remain attributable as an assertion, but its assessed status is unestablished and it cannot be offered as a justified favorable decision for current reliance.

## Method definitions: FUNC-027 / AR-062

A method definition contains the selected criteria, intended claim scope, exact subject/state, technique, conditions, expected observations and authoring provenance. Explicitly distinguish a condition declared irrelevant with rationale from an omitted condition. Criteria and expected observations must be sufficiently specific for the assessor to recognize positive and adverse outcomes; a generic instruction to run tests cannot establish a complete method for an unidentified criterion.

Preparation checks each required component and produces complete-for-declared-scope or incomplete-with-reasons information. Neither means performed or passed. The method can name a manual technique; automated execution is optional. Changing the expected observations, criterion basis or subject binding creates a new identified content basis. Existing observations retain the method basis actually used. Retain its exact relevant text or a retrievable immutable reference where required to interpret relied-on observations; an updated mutable path alone is insufficient.

## Occurrences and observations: FUNC-028 / AR-063

An occurrence identifies the attempted method and basis, actual subject/state and conditions, observing actor/source, occurrence identity and supporting evidence. Expected observations and actual observations are separate fields in the logical content. The occurrence distinguishes planned or not performed, attempted but unable to assess, interrupted with a declared assessed/unassessed boundary, and completed assessment. Criterion outcomes separately identify actual favorable, adverse or inconclusive observations. A completed procedure may observe a criterion failure; a process exit error may mean inability to assess. The assessor must explain that distinction instead of inferring it from one exit code.

An observation records what was actually obtained and how it supports or contradicts specified criteria. A planned check cannot contain claimed actual observations. For interruption, preserve attributable results already obtained and name the unassessed remainder; those results do not cover it. Missing exact state, conditions, provenance or required evidence remains a reliance gap even when the actor reports success. An evidence path whose bytes are unavailable is an unavailable reference, not retained proof.

When recording a later favorable observation, retain contradictory or adverse content and its original provenance while it remains relevant to current reliance or unresolved obligations. A resolution must name the contradiction, accountable decision, reason and affected scope. Comparable working summaries may be replaced under the selected record contract, but replacement must carry the contrary meaning forward or refer to retained content until it has an explicit disposition. No universal archive of all attempts is required. A later occurrence does not rewrite the earlier occurrence's state or convert a failure to execute into a failed criterion.

## Applicability and coverage: FUNC-029/030 / AR-064/065

Normalize the proposed claim into exact subject/state, included criteria, relevant conditions, gate purpose and scope, and governing freshness/deferral rules. Preserve the selected evidence population and its acquisition limitations. For each offered observation compare subject binding, criterion basis, scope, method/conditions, provenance, evidence availability and any required freshness. Return concrete matches, mismatches and unknowns. Content-identity equality establishes only the defined comparison; the accountable assessor supplies the engineering rationale for relevant conditions and semantic applicability.

For each included criterion, expose offered applicable support, contrary observations, missing support, unknown applicability and contradiction disposition. A structurally complete list of references is not semantic sufficiency. A passing label, stable entity identity or retained method alone establishes none. A declared limited evidence population cannot silently imply discovery of all relevant contrary evidence; identified omissions restrict the conclusion. Unknown materiality or missing dependency information remains uncertain and potentially affects the claim.

Keep requirement review, integrated Design, advisory advice, whole-change Code Review and final Verify distinct. Requirement review may accept an assessable reuse basis before Function or AR completion. Design review addresses that completion; a narrower advisory inspection cannot establish a whole-change judgment. The selected review policy also determines independence: identify actual contributors, assessor and concrete separation evidence. An actor ID or role label alone does not establish independence.

IF-010 supplies these analyses to its existing consumers using the same meanings. Its consumer retains release qualification or workflow continuation. Support reuse honors explicit freshness requirements and nondeferable duties. A permitted tradeoff records a decision; it does not create an observation of satisfaction or execution authority.

## Judgments: FUNC-031 / AR-065

A judgment identifies claim purpose, exact included and excluded scope, subject/criterion basis, accountable assessor, independence where required, reasoning, applicable supporting and contrary observations, limitations and unresolved obligations. Each criterion in a favorable satisfaction claim requires a traceable semantic explanation of why its observations suffice. Missing required support or an unresolved material contradiction leaves that scope unestablished. A narrower judgment is prepared only when the assessor explicitly supplies the narrower claim and its exclusions; the system cannot silently shrink an unsuccessful full claim.

Reject a claim of justified full satisfaction that conflicts with known uncovered criteria, missing required independent provenance or unresolved material contradictions. Preserve the supplied assertion and the inspection diagnostics where useful; do not manufacture a substitute judgment. Unknown semantic sufficiency returns an unresolved question for the responsible assessor. A complete machine coverage table cannot supply the judgment. Keep accepted exceptions and authority decisions separately labeled and traceable.

Retention records the actual assessor's conclusion, not the preparer's approval. Updating work progress, collecting attachments or routing a task cannot author reviewer approval. After successful recording, a reader can inspect the judgment's exact assessed basis and selected support. A saved response reports persistence only; neither command nor storage establishes adequacy or progression.

## Reassessment and selective retention: AR-066

Reassessment begins with an identified currently relied-on claim and changes to its subject, criteria, conditions, evidence, dependencies or governing rules. Compare the recorded basis with the new inspected basis, keeping actor-reported no-change distinct from a defined mechanical comparison and stating each scope. Timestamps and existing current labels establish no comparison. An impact path is a candidate for inspection, not proof that all dependencies are known or that evidence is invalid.

The responsible assessor classifies affected criteria and interactions, support that remains usable with explicit rationale, verification that needs repetition and unresolved impact. Changed bytes alone do not decide semantic applicability. Demonstrably immaterial edits or equivalent evidence-collection retries may receive a concise applicability disposition under Assessment without another review. Material changes require affected reassessment; incomplete dependencies expose the potentially affected scope and cannot justify no-impact. Retain the unaffected support's original attribution and basis; do not retarget its old observations to changed content.

Planned repeated verification supplies no new observations. Current support remains unestablished until applicable observations and an accountable judgment cover the affected scope. A replacement current judgment names its new basis, reasoning, limitations and assessor. Preserve unresolved findings and any prior conclusion still used. Superseded details without decision value may be compacted only after their relied-on meaning is preserved or their reliance ends; do not retain every intermediate report by default.

A completed Change remains a historical acceptance account. A later regression becomes linked new work. A discovered mistake in the original account receives an explicit attributable annotation preserving the declared historical meaning; neither ongoing comparison nor silent historical rewrite is appropriate.

## Retention cooperation and failure

For governed v2/v4 recording, MOD-012 maps the prepared content to supported Evidence, Review, Verification, Basis and work tasks using the selected command/record schemas. Method definitions and criterion-level content not represented as native fields are retained as supporting attachments or structured prose under the relevant Evidence account; this design does not advertise a native Verification-definition entity, new task or machine-interpreted assurance payload. Use supported attachment selection to read the retained bytes. The semantic attachment remains inspectable by its assessor; no generic parser or automatic inference is assumed. The operational record retains the accountable scope, summary, limitations and attachment references required to interpret that support.

A method-only v4 Evidence carrier uses result `inconclusive` for the unperformed target verification. Its observation records the honest reported or unknown basis for the supplied method; it contains no claimed criterion-execution observation. The procedure, scope, summary and limitations explicitly identify retained planning content, no execution and no established satisfaction. Attach the exact method content needed for later interpretation. A successful carrier save is not a passed technique. A separately performed review of method quality has its own actual observation and assessed scope; it cannot be relabeled as execution of the planned target verification.

Choose retained content whenever an identity/reference alone would not preserve the meaning needed for reliance. Bind attachment content and the supplied statement to the selected subject basis; a mere filename does not establish provenance. Caller-reported assessment content is distinct from MOD-011's actual read observations. Existing IF-003 guarantees of project association, expected revision, structural candidate validation, atomic selected publication and retained attachment identity remain authoritative. Reuse the existing IR-008 command/storage ARs; these do not make storage the owner of evidence sufficiency.

Preparation is not a reservation. Before mutation, check the current revision and relevant subject observations and submit the same prepared semantic content. A revision conflict or relevant subject change requires a fresh read and proportionate reassessment of the intended update; no blind retry. A definitely uncommitted failure leaves the original account authoritative. After interruption or lost response, use supported scoped reads to determine actual effects, compare the submitted content/basis and report confirmed, uncommitted or unresolved retention. Do not claim saved from the attempt, or failed retention merely because the receipt was lost. Busy, inaccessible attachments, incomplete reads and unsupported record contracts remain explicit limits. No fallback store, inferred format migration, background retry or hidden external read is allowed.

If the assessor resolves a question by changing the semantic content, claim scope, contradiction disposition or inspected basis, MOD-012 returns that candidate through IF-014 preparation before recording. Only unchanged admitted content may proceed directly from the prior preparation. Repreparation checks the new candidate and its limits; it neither renews approval nor substitutes for any required independent reassessment.

For standalone use, identify the selected artifact, accountable writer, original content identity and observed write result, and expose unavailable or conflicting retention. A missing chosen retention mechanism prevents reporting a retained method/observation/judgment. Standalone availability does not adopt operational commands or waive required review policy.

## Realization and view composition

The selected realization is assessor-assisted local content preparation, not a new automatic inference service. MOD-007's method/observation/applicability/coverage/judgment procedures are published as guidance resources consumed by MOD-012's existing specialist skill execution. An accountable human or agent applies these procedures and records its actual reasoning. Existing CLI JavaScript and MOD-011 SQLite/attachment storage supply operational retention; no new database, runtime language, network service, worker, mandatory CI or verification scheduler is needed. The browser consumes separately selected judgments and cannot perform this assessment on behalf of its reader.

Development ownership places semantic source instructions with the responsible specialist methods and skills, packaged under existing MOD-013 resource production. MOD-012 adapts prepared content to current task schemas; it does not duplicate the semantic policy in CLI or storage. Current packages are not certified by this proposed realization. Publishing refined guidance, adopting a typed automatic assurance format or adding a runtime analysis engine would require separately reviewed concrete delivery. This bounded design requires none of those technologies to express or carry out an accountable manual assessment.

Physical placement is the assessor's local workspace/process, explicitly selected project operational store and optional retained attachments. An external test tool supplies attributable observations only through the assessor's selected input; no implicit network fetch or trust in an evidence URL is introduced. Current observations may be read through the supported local CLI. Historical accounts and external files remain within their stated availability limits. Store transaction scope does not extend to tool execution or external engineering files.

The [responsibility and interaction diagrams](README.md#assurance-responsibilities) show the Logical and Process questions. The preceding paragraphs define Development and Physical composition. SCN-026 prepares a complete or explicitly incomplete method without observations; SCN-027 records actual, interrupted or unable-to-assess occurrences; SCN-028 composes applicability, coverage and an accountable supported/narrow/unestablished judgment; SCN-029 reassesses changed current reliance while preserving historical completion. These Scenario walkthroughs retain the original black-box obligations.
