# Baselines and controlled state changes

[MOD-017](module.json) owns this composition for SR-020–024. MOD-005 owns baseline identity, scope, comparison and recovery through FUNC-020–022; MOD-006 owns transition meaning and retirement disposition through FUNC-023/024. MOD-001 retains engineering content; MOD-003 interprets and checks it. This is proposed Design, not an implemented baseline engine, executed recovery or permission to remove material.

## Scope and cooperation

A baseline is an explicitly retained engineering state, not a current operational handoff, database backup or implicit copy of every work session. Its recoverability is bounded by its declared content and intact retained storage. SR-006/007 and the existing Workflow govern resumption and action authority; SR-074–077 govern operational backup, restore, transfer and migration separately. This slice covers five of IR-003's eleven SRs and supplies no complete IR-003 assessment.

MOD-005 first consumes read-only IF-007 from MOD-016 to inspect an exact selected state. New IF-015 supplies engineering-content custody and isolated recovery from the same parent; MOD-001 contributes through internal IF-001 and MOD-003 through IF-002. IF-007 remains read-only. Inspection, interpretation, conformance, accountable coherence, retention and authorization remain separate outcomes.

MOD-006 provides IF-016 controlled-state preparation to MOD-005 and, exposed through MOD-017, to MOD-012. It reuses FUNC-026's selected-policy authority interpretation, also used by IF-009, without changing release authority or inventing a second policy. Its prepared transition and retirement accounts are supplied by the responsible participant. MOD-012 arranges explicit operational recording through IF-004; MOD-010/011 retain admission and custody through IF-003. No direct Governance database call is introduced. MOD-006 consumes IF-015 only for expressly selected model-content retirement, not operational-store mutation.

Unknown operation selectors, ambiguous project or subject identities, escaped paths and unsupported selected contracts reject before dependent checks. A well-identified but incomplete basis yields explicit gaps and no complete favorable result. Logical operation names and result distinctions here are not a new CLI, wire schema or installed capability.

## Baseline content and establishment — MOD-005

The participant identifies the project, exact source state, complete intended membership, selected retention policy, purpose and provenance. MOD-005 assigns a unique baseline identity in that project's catalog; identity is separate from its exact content identity. A descriptor binds membership, authoritative definitions and relationships, original metamodel/profile/rules, recorded rationale and provenance, included realization/assurance material, external-only references, scope exclusions and the custody receipt. Same identity with different descriptor content is a conflict, never an overwrite.

Inspect a coherent source through IF-007. Original rules must be available and applicable; newer defaults cannot repair a missing historical basis. Have MOD-016 apply the supported full rule selection through MOD-003 to that exact captured scope before claiming structural conformance. Coherence also includes the participant's accountable assessment of declared scope, necessary context, unresolved conflicts and cross-material consistency; a mechanical pass alone is insufficient. An unavailable required rule, missing member or unresolved required coherence question withholds establishment as a complete coherent baseline. Partial diagnostic material can be retained with its limits and must not masquerade as an established baseline.

Included realization or assurance material arrives as explicitly supplied immutable bytes or supported immutable references with actual acquisition observations, source owner, meaning, identity and inclusion policy. It is not fetched from an arbitrary link or inferred from the model. Mutable operational records must be coherently exported by their existing custody owner and supplied with exact observations; a database filename alone is not an immutable export. Preserve assessment subjects, conclusions and applicability as historical facts. Unsupported or unavailable required material prevents a complete claim; a declared external-only reference remains a reference and supplies no content recovery promise.

MOD-005 obtains the applicable action basis through IF-016. Existing standing authority is reusable within its conditions; merely establishing a baseline need not introduce an extra approval gate. Before retention the exact selection, policy, authority conditions and relevant coherence basis must still match. A changed current source does not mutate an already captured immutable basis; retain that named earlier state only when the request authorizes it, otherwise reconcile the request. Never silently substitute the newest state.

Submit exact captured material and manifest through IF-015. Obtain confirmed durable custody for every required member and the manifest, then publish the descriptor in MOD-005's durable catalog. A descriptor is established only after the custody receipt and catalog readback agree on baseline identity, complete membership and content. A catalog-write failure can leave preserved but unregistered content; report that fact and withhold establishment. An inspection response or successful staging operation is not retention confirmation.

## Immutable custody and interrupted establishment — MOD-001

IF-015 composes IF-001's new `retain_baseline_content` and `read_baseline_content` operations. Custody never chooses baseline scope, approval or lifecycle. Retain the exact manifest and member bytes in a private append-only content area separate from mutable current definitions. Include profile and rule content, not merely a name or a reference to the current profile. Content identity binds the manifest and membership as well as each member; path, kind, length, relevant file attributes and link representation are explicit when needed for selected meaning. No live symlink, hardlink to mutable input or path escape can replace retained bytes. Unsupported file/material kinds are reported before complete retention.

The proposed local filesystem realization stages an owned bundle, verifies every member and the complete manifest, durably flushes data/metadata, then publishes a sealed bundle using supported no-replace atomic rename and directory durability. A per-project custody exclusion prevents competing publication for the same baseline binding. Existing identical content may be reused only after exact identity and integrity comparison; a same-name different bundle conflicts. A catalog descriptor references a sealed immutable bundle. Preserve original bytes; normalized parsed data are derived indexes, not the recovered originals.

Publish the catalog descriptor with its own guarded durable write only after bundle confirmation. No cross-owner transaction is promised: sealed-but-unregistered, pending descriptor and confirmed descriptor remain distinguishable. On restart or lost response inspect the exact request identity, sealed manifest, content and catalog state before declaring retained or retrying. A stable request binding helps reconcile effects but grants no exactly-once execution or permission. Incomplete staging can be discarded only when owned by that attempt and unreferenced; never delete the sole confirmed bundle to make a failed catalog write look rolled back.

The adapter must qualify its filesystem exclusion, atomic publication and flush semantics. If those cannot be established, it must report unsupported durability and withhold a complete preservation claim. Noncooperating external mutation is not prevented by advisory locks; detected inconsistency or loss of reliable custody makes affected results unavailable/unconfirmed. Intact retained storage is the stated recovery assumption, not a disaster-recovery service.

## Comparison — MOD-005

Select two baseline identities and explicit comparison scope. Resolve each immutable descriptor and custody bundle independently; validate identities, membership integrity and original interpretation basis. Obtain each interpretation through IF-015, where MOD-003 decodes the captured retained content under its own original rules. Duplicate or ambiguous identities remain findings; never choose a matching display name to invent continuity.

Match governed entities by stable identity. Expose additions, removals, unchanged identities, changed authoritative fields, relationship types/endpoints, retirement/lifecycle facts, titles and locations. A rename or move remains the same entity. Declare direction, before/after values and exact source bases. Report changes to applicable rules and relevant provenance even where represented definition bytes are unchanged.

An addition or removal requires established membership on both sides. Partial acquisition cannot turn unknown members into absence. When interpretation cannot be related, report the known raw difference and unsupported semantic comparison separately; never infer equivalence from hashes or names. No recorded difference means only the inspected selected facts under the identified bases; it is neither semantic equivalence nor approval or satisfaction. A comparison does not rewrite either descriptor, relationship, decision or rule.

## Recovery — MOD-005 and content custody

Recovery selects one identified intact baseline and its recorded scope. MOD-005 validates its descriptor and invokes IF-015's `recover_baseline_content`, composed with IF-001's exact retained reads and isolated materialization. The destination is a newly created private output area; an existing or active-work destination is rejected. The supplied caller cannot redirect a manifest path outside that area. Storage owns only its created output; cancellation cleanup must not remove unrelated or pre-existing material.

Copy the manifest, authoritative definitions and relationship facts, original interpretation rules, recorded provenance and all included material without rewriting identity, allocation or historical assessment meaning. Hash/length/inventory verification checks exact bytes and the supported recorded attributes; interpret under the retained rules and report fidelity and interpretation separately. Complete recovery requires full declared content fidelity and an available original interpretation basis. Corrupt, missing, unsupported, inaccessible or only partly materialized scope remains explicit, and a byte copy with uninterpretable rules cannot claim complete meaning-preserving recovery.

MOD-005 compares the output inventory/content with the sealed baseline and reports the exact recovered scope, exclusions and result. Later unsaved work and unretained external content are never implied recovered. A lost receipt requires inspection of the owned output and original request, not a claim of failure or permission to overwrite. Failed/canceled recovery leaves the original baseline and active model untouched. Report partial output truthfully; only owned temporary output may be removed.

Recovery produces an inspectable independent state. Adopting it over active work is a different, explicitly authorized action with its own writer and safeguards. This design does not introduce a whole-model adoption writer, turn IR-005's migration candidate into an adopted state, or route model recovery through operational backup/restore. Existing change/adoption policy remains authoritative.

## Controlled transition provenance — MOD-006

IF-016 prepares an attributable transition account from an identified Change/project, initiating need and governing basis, exact before/result baseline references, affected subjects and revisions, actual participants/actions/decisions, authority basis and observed disposition. Proposed intent, observed execution, recorded decision and confirmed retained outcome remain distinct. Missing input is not fabricated from a favorable review or next-action suggestion.

An established transition requires both baseline descriptors and retained custody to be confirmed, the actual adopted result to be observed, and the accountable decision basis to be recorded. A successful command or matching candidate is not proof of adoption. Without this basis retain proposed or incomplete status and explicit missing context; never invent a resulting baseline. If actual engineering effects occurred but operational recording failed, report those separate facts and withhold a fully recorded transition claim. This operation records meaning; it does not execute adoption or renew review applicability.

MOD-012 carries prepared content through existing IF-004/v2/v4 tasks. Use native Change/rationale/basis/work fields where they have the required meaning and retain the exact baseline descriptors, transition explanation and observations as supporting attachments where the native schema has no field. A supporting Evidence carrier describes actual supplied observations and their limits; unassessed transition support remains inconclusive. Do not invent a native Baseline record, mark verification passed to store an attachment, or equate an operational Basis with an engineering Baseline. The selected original attachment bytes remain available via supported reads.

The semantic account must identify the project/Change, immutable before/result bases, adopted outcome, source rationale, actors and limitations so it remains inspectable without replaying working updates. Current definitions remain at their owners and require no Change replay. Completed operational Changes may preserve a compact acceptance account, not every implementation action or intermediate check. Corrections to significant adopted decisions/completion statements add attributable explanatory content referencing the original basis; no silent historical overwrite. Superseded routine working summaries can be replaced under current-handoff rules when surviving reliance is preserved.

Before recording, compare the current operational revision and relevant observed subjects with preparation. Changed semantic content or material basis requires fresh IF-016 preparation and applicable independent reassessment; a stale write is not blindly replayed. Unknown effects after interruption require exact scoped readback. Missing historical content is reported as unavailable; it cannot be replaced with newer bytes or treated as proof no Change happened. Existing operational transaction scope does not include model custody or execution of a governed action.

## Retirement and current reliance — MOD-006

Classify each proposed removal using the actual policy and current reliance: engineering definitions/baselines/explicitly preserved history require a recoverable original basis; superseded operational summaries and routine logs need no archival copy if no open obligation, current conclusion or retention decision relies on them. Age, a successful import or a file being committed alone establishes no permission or adequate recovery. An uncommitted required original needs explicit retention before removal; a commit reference requires actual exact recoverability, including required content outside Git.

The participant supplies a bounded inventory of affected material, current consumers, active work, unresolved findings, evidence/support references, surviving obligations and required historical recovery. MOD-006 evaluates an attributable disposition for each: retain, replace/transfer with identified current owner and valid references, or explicitly retire within policy. Use exact observed definitions and complete relevant incoming relationships; model traversal alone does not discover unmodeled file readers, prose links or operational reliance. Inspect those consumers explicitly. Unknown inventory completeness or unresolved reliance withholds readiness for the affected removal.

Replacements must be present, readable and semantically adequate for the surviving obligation before removal. Required current operation must not fetch retired definitions. Preserve relevant contrary evidence and failures for other scopes when replacing comparable working support. A later favorable check for one subject cannot erase an unresolved failure for another. Human disposition and justification remain accountable; mechanically finding no link does not decide usefulness.

Assess applicable authority for the exact removal, project, subjects, expected state and conditions. A still-valid standing grant suffices; missing, revoked or changed authority withholds the reserved action. Releasing a retained baseline/history bundle is a separate retention-policy decision and dependency review, never ordinary current-view cleanup. This slice does not implement history expiry or garbage collection of retained bundles.

Before any removal, the responsible execution participant acquires and holds an explicit maintenance interval covering every relevant model, operational and external-file dependency writer, including sources of applicable policy/authority and replacement content. MOD-006 identifies that scope during preparation; the executor establishes actual exclusive custody and records its basis. The initial local realization uses participant-controlled quiescence: stop or suspend other agents/tools that can modify the selected model, operational store, required recovery/replacement content or reliance inputs, and retain exclusive control of those write surfaces. A compatible adapter may instead supply bounded exclusions held across all those owners. A timestamp, a once-read operational revision, a model-only lock or an unsupported assertion that no other writer exists is insufficient. If any required writer or authority source cannot be placed under that stable boundary, withhold the affected removal; no new operational command or existing CLI support for a global lock is presumed.

While that interval is held, perform the final exact-content, reliance, authority and recovery checks, apply only the selected effects, inspect the resulting current dependencies, and record or otherwise preserve the actual outcome and remaining obligations before releasing the interval. The participant may perform its own expected scoped operational recording under the interval, reconciling that update rather than treating it as an external race. Loss of exclusive custody stops further effects and makes the affected conclusion unconfirmed; preserve the actual partial outcome and a durable blocked-scope handoff. On interruption, later writers must honor that blocked scope until explicit inspection/reconciliation under newly established quiescence. Do not automatically unlock and resume dependent work while integrity is unresolved. A failed final recording does not undo removal; retain the accountable recovery/handoff information and withhold completed-retirement reporting. This is an explicit execution precondition, not a distributed transaction or an implemented lock service.

For current model definitions, IF-015 `retire_current_model_material` delegates to IF-001 `remove_retired_model_material`: receive exact removal/replacement inventory, expected state, complete affected resulting-model checks, required recovery evidence, accepted reliance disposition and applicable authority. Within the held maintenance interval, hold the existing model write exclusion, recheck all relevant observed bases at publication and bind MOD-003 checks to the exact resulting candidate. External state changes or expired preconditions require reconciliation, not continued deletion. Stage supported replacements before removal and persist the unresolved-write marker before the first authoritative effect; reuse existing relationship-publication safety. Partial publication blocks coherent affected-model reads and further writes until actual effects are inspected and reconciled. Report retained/replaced/removed/unresolved members without inventing rollback. Never remove retained bundles or alter historical references.

For operational detail, MOD-012 uses existing selected record/attachment retention tasks through IF-004; those tasks preserve their own revision, unresolved-reference and closed-history guarantees. For obsolete operational source files or other material outside model custody, the maintainer holds the same cross-custody maintenance interval and performs the explicitly authorized filesystem cleanup with the same exact inventory, immediately checked expected bytes/reliance and recovery bases, and reports actual effects through IF-016 preparation/recording. No automatic cross-store deletion or universal filesystem executor is introduced. Stop on mismatch or partial failure, preserve unrelated files and disclose remaining broken dependencies. Successful import alone does not settle original retention or authorize removal.

The resulting account names actual effects, unaffected and unresolved scope, required recovery locations and any intentionally discarded disposable detail. Later reads of discarded information report its unavailability honestly. Completion requires current references and obligations to work after cleanup; proposed disposition or passing preflight alone is not completed retirement.

## Realization and acceptance intent

The proposed local Rust model engine supplies baseline descriptor orchestration in MOD-005, immutable filesystem custody in MOD-001, profile/checking reuse in MOD-003 and comparison/recovery components. No persistent inverse graph or new database is needed. Versioned manifest/profile adapters retain exact original bytes and supported material semantics; concrete representation and platform qualification belong to subsequent delivery. MOD-006's semantic transition/retirement preparation is participant-assisted guidance, as with assurance preparation; MOD-012 adapts actual content to existing JavaScript CLI/SQLite operational recording. No network fetch, background cleanup, automatic approval service or new public command is selected.

Physically, mutable current definitions, sealed retained bundles/catalog and isolated recovery output are distinct custody areas. Operational Change records remain in their separately selected store. Their transactions are not a whole-project transaction. Local process exclusion and durable markers handle supported model writes; unsupported platforms must expose their limitation. Storage destruction, unsaved work and unretained external material are outside the recovery guarantee.

The responsibility and sequence views in [the composition README](README.md#baseline-state-responsibilities) show Logical and Process concerns. These realization paragraphs define Development and Physical choices.
