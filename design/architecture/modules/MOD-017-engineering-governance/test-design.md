# Change and quality control: adoption integration test design

Apply the [shared test rules](../../../support/test-design/rules.md). MOD-017 owns the adoption decision composed from work, authority, assurance and external package/installation observations. Canonical Scenario records remain black-box; the following is an internal cooperation walkthrough and future proof intent.

| Scenario / basis | Cooperation and representative observation | Plausible defect and required proof boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SCN-082; SR-083; SR-023/024/055 | IF-011 prepares scoped work disposition from package and record facts; installation partially fails or another actor changes the basis; activation remains unset and history is intact. | Mixed contracts advertised as active, unrelated Changes forced into migration, or relabeled historic approval. Use real selected installation units and operational transaction boundary when implemented; compare retained original bytes and actual current contract independently. |

For the SR-083 recording-dependency refinement, compare these independent observations:

| Selected case and variation | Expected observation and defect exposed |
| --- | --- |
| New project with established absence of earlier work; contrast an unreadable legacy store | Preparation accepts the former with an attributable empty work inventory, target and project authority, without inventing a prior contract/Change or initializing storage. It needs no historical import after other prerequisites hold; the unreadable case remains an unresolved dependency. Empty target storage cannot stand for inspected absence. |
| Compatible existing records with retained review support; contrast missing authority or a changed dependency | Activation can proceed only on the current coherent basis. Compatibility or successful installation alone cannot provide authority or renew a judgment. |
| Selected work relies on incompatible earlier records; migration is unavailable, fails or has an uncertain outcome | Affected adoption remains blocked, actual prior authority or unavailability is explicit, and original work/subjects survive. Starting an empty store or omitting the depended-on record cannot bypass the block. |
| Necessary migration completes under its qualified contract; unrelated legacy work remains outside selection | Reconcile exact migrated support and fresh authority before guarded activation. Preserve unrelated work without automatic import, and expose partial scope instead of project-wide success. |

These are Design acceptance observations, not executed migration evidence. Storage and installation retain their own qualification and preservation duties.

Exercise a competing local actor between inspected basis and activation, unknown contract values, selected versus unrelated Changes, partial replacement, and interruption before and after durable publication. Observe actual contract availability, preserved original bytes and explicit activation disposition independently of a successful installation return value. Reuse an earlier contract only when all its required components are intact and compatible. No current suite is claimed to establish these target outcomes.

[Operations test design](../MOD-018-engineering-operations/test-design.md) owns SCN-075–081 and cross-boundary handoff/assurance observations. Child conformance does not establish the parent adoption outcome. Concrete commands and execution allocation belong to Delivery.

The [MOD-014 replacement groups](../MOD-019-product-delivery/modules/MOD-014-verified-skill-installation/README.md#verification-intent-and-decision) own actual per-unit installation and SCN-063 retirement effects. The [MOD-013 production groups](../MOD-019-product-delivery/modules/MOD-013-product-package-production/README.md#verification-intent) own candidate/old-client compatibility. At this parent boundary, combine successful candidate qualification with unavailable v4 recording, or partial per-Change activation: neither establishes project-wide completion. Preserve unselected work and require fresh reconciliation after competing activation.
