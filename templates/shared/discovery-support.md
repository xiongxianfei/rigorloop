# Discovery support contract

An explicit invocation creates or explicitly revises one standalone Git-tracked supporting artifact. Identify the topic, supported decision, owning stage or change when known, inputs examined, important assumptions, remaining uncertainty, and recommended next owner.

Own only the supporting artifact. Do not approve the supported direction, progress lifecycle state, or edit another stage's artifact unless separately invoked with that stage's authority. A conclusion affects a governed decision only when its owner adopts or relies on it through the owner's normal artifact and review path.

Distinguish established facts from inference and assumptions. Preserve attributable evidence and confidence where factual claims matter. Do not record secrets, credentials, unnecessary private raw input, or machine-local absolute paths.

Stop when additional work is unlikely to affect the supported decision, required evidence or authority is unavailable, scope would expand materially, the artifact target or required resource is unsafe or ambiguous, or owner judgment is required. A stopped operation grants no completion or downstream authority and must not leave an unrelated artifact overwritten.

When a conclusion contradicts an approved upstream decision, record the contradiction and route it to that decision's owner. Do not silently change the approved artifact. Hand the completed support artifact to the identified owner to adopt, reject, qualify, or investigate further.
